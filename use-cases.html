Use cases

A logo for whatever you're starting

A new company, a weekend project, a client deadline — the brief is different every time. Here's how people put LogoMinter to work.

Who it's for

Early-stage startups

Get a credible brand mark on day one so your landing page, deck, and demo don't ship with a placeholder. Revisit and refine as the product finds its shape.

Small & local businesses

Cafes, studios, trades, and shops that need a clean logo for signage, menus, and socials — without a designer's timeline or invoice.

Creators & personal brands

A wordmark and avatar that read well as a channel icon, on merch, and across every profile you post to.

Side projects & indie apps

Ship the thing without stalling on branding. Generate a mark that looks intentional and get back to building.

Events & communities

A one-off identity for a meetup, newsletter, podcast, or pop-up that still looks considered.

Agencies & freelancers

Spin up directions fast to explore territory with a client, then refine the one that resonates instead of starting from a blank artboard.

Where the logo ends up

Because every export ships in high-resolution PNG and vector-ready formats with light and dark variants, one generation covers most of what a new brand needs.

On the web

Site header, favicon, social profile pictures, and Open Graph cards.

In print

Business cards, packaging, menus, flyers, and signage that need clean scaling.

On products

Merch, stickers, app icons, and anywhere a small, legible mark has to work hard.

A typical flow

  1. Explore directions

    Generate a set of concepts to see the range — sometimes the strongest idea isn't the one you'd have described.

  2. Narrow and refine

    Pick a favorite and iterate on palette, type, and mark until it feels unmistakably yours.

  3. Export everywhere

    Download the full set of formats and drop your logo into your site, socials, and print in one go.
