Questions, answered

The things people ask most before making their first logo. Still stuck? Reach us any time and a human will get back to you.

The basics

How long does it take to make a logo?

Your first set of concepts arrives in about a minute. From there, most people spend a few minutes refining before they land on the one they want to download.

Do I need any design experience?

None at all. You describe your brand in plain language and pick what you like. If you do have opinions about type, color, or style, there's room to steer — but it's optional.

How many concepts do I get?

Each generation returns several genuinely different directions rather than one idea in a few colors, so you're choosing between real options from the first screen.

Can I make changes after generating?

Yes. Pick a concept and refine it as many times as you need — change the palette, adjust the type, simplify the mark. Each revision keeps what you liked and changes only what you asked for.

Files & usage

What file formats do I get?

High-resolution PNGs for the web and vector-ready files for print and scaling, each in light and dark variants so your logo works on any background.

Do I own the logo I create?

You're free to use the logo you download across your website, social profiles, and products. See our terms for the specifics on rights and usage.

Will my logo look good at small sizes?

Logos are built to hold up from a browser favicon to a printed sign. If a mark feels too busy at small sizes, ask for a simpler version and re-export.

Can I use it commercially?

Yes — LogoMinter is built for real businesses. Full commercial terms are covered in our legal page.

Billing & support

What does it cost?

Pricing is shown on the homepage before you commit. You'll always see the price for what you're making up front — no surprise charges.

What if I'm not happy with the results?

Refining usually gets you there, but if something isn't right, get in touch and we'll make it right. We'd rather you leave with a logo you actually want to use.

Is there an API?

Yes — a developer API is in private preview. See the API documentation to learn more or request a key.

How do I reach a person?

Message us on Instagram or through the contact options in our resources. Real replies, not a bot.

Didn't find your answer?

Try it yourself — most questions answer themselves once you see your first concepts.
