Write a better brief

The clearer your input, the sharper the concepts. A few habits that reliably raise the quality of the first screen:

Say what you do, plainly

"A neighborhood coffee roaster" gives the model more to work with than "coffee brand." Concrete beats vague.

Name a feeling, not just a style

Words like calm, bold, playful, or premium steer the mood. Add a reference era — vintage, modern — if you have one.

Refine one thing at a time

Change the palette, then the type, then the mark. Small, specific asks keep what's already working intact.

Test it small

Preview your favorite at favicon size early — the strongest marks stay legible when they shrink.
